Add it to Gmail

  1. Gmail: Settings (gear) → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new. Name it "Main".
  2. Click Copy above, click into the signature box and paste (Cmd+V or Ctrl+V).
  3. Do the same for the short one and name it "Short".
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick the address hello@mariape.co: "For new emails use" Main, "On reply/forward use" Short.
  5. Scroll down and click Save changes.

If the Copy button doesn't work in your browser, drag to select the signature in the white box and press Cmd+C or Ctrl+C.
