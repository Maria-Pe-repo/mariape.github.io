FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does SEO take?+

Fixes can help within weeks, but steady growth usually takes several months. Anyone who promises page one by next week is guessing.

Do you guarantee rankings?+

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings. I guarantee the work, and I report what it changes.

Do I need a new website first?+

Not always. If your site is sound, we fix and build on it. If it's holding you back, a website plan may be the better start.

What is a Google Business Profile?+

It's your free listing on Google Search and Maps, with your hours, reviews and photos. For local businesses it's often the first thing customers see.
