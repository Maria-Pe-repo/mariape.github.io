FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I own my website?+

Your domain, logo, photos and copy are yours from day one. After 24 months on a plan, the site's design and code are yours too, whether you stay or leave.

What if I want to leave?+

After the 12-month minimum, plans run month to month with 30 days' notice. You can also buy the site out early.

Is the mock-up really free?+

Yes. Send your website and I'll send back a homepage mock-up with notes on what I'd change. There's no cost and no obligation to go ahead.

What is the founding rate?+

My first 5 website clients get the Growth plan at a founding price for their first 12 months. In exchange, I ask for a short case study and a Google review once the site is live.

Can I edit the site myself?+

Yes. Sites are built on WordPress, so you can update text and photos. Or send me the changes and I'll do them within your monthly hours.

I have a Wix or Squarespace site. Can you redo it?+

Yes. I rebuild it on WordPress and bring your content across, so you can edit it yourself and it isn't tied to one platform.

Do I need professional photos?+

Real photos of your work and team beat stock, and phone photos are fine to start. If yours aren't ready, I'll use suitable stock until they are.

How long does it take?+

About 2 weeks for Launch and about 4 weeks for Growth, once I have your content and photos.

Can you also run my social media or SEO?+

Yes. Growth already includes SEO and AI search set-up. Social media, ongoing SEO and email can be added, run from the same plan and voice.

Why monthly instead of one price?+

You avoid a large upfront bill, and your site keeps getting looked after instead of going stale after launch.
