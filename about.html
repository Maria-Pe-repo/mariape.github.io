About

Authenticity + Strategy.

I'm Maria Lourdes Plaza Pe, a web and content consultant from Metro Manila with around 8 years in marketing. I help small businesses sound like themselves, with a clear plan behind every post, page and email.

Hi, I'm Maria.

My mission is simple: create content that starts real conversations and leads to real results, with a clear message on every platform. I want the brands I work with to become trusted voices in their space, through strategy and storytelling that fit who they are.

Today I lead content and design at Roger Roger, a digital marketing agency, and I built the system that keeps every client's work on brief. Before that, I ran regional marketing for business software across the Philippines and Indonesia for four years.

On my own, I take on a small number of small business clients in the US, Australia and the Philippines: websites, SEO and AI search, email and social.

I studied accountancy and then marketing management at the University of Santo Tomas, so I care about the numbers as much as the words.
