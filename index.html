How I work

I listen first.
Then we build.

The same four steps on every project, so nothing gets missed.

01

Review

I audit your website, socials and search presence the way a customer would, and note what's worth keeping.

02

Plan and write

We agree a clear plan. Then I write every page in your voice, built on real facts and search data.

03

Design and launch

You approve the design before anything goes live. Then I build it on a site you can edit yourself.

04

Improve

Each month I make your changes and report what's working: visits, enquiries, search and AI visibility, and what we do next.

Behind the scenes

AI does the heavy lifting. I make the calls.

I designed my own AI workflow for research, design, search, copy and web development, the same kind of system I build and run as a content system lead. It works from current trends and my own quality rules, and nothing reaches you until I've reviewed it.
