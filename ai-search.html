FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is AI search different from SEO?+

It builds on SEO. Google says its AI features use the same foundations as regular search, so the fixes overlap. What's new is checking and tracking what AI tools say about you.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?+

No, and be wary of anyone who does. I can make your business easier to understand and cite, and track whether it's mentioned.

Do I need special AI files like llms.txt?+

Not for Google. Google says no special files or markup are needed to appear in its AI features. I won't sell you one as a fix.

How do you measure it?+

Monthly checks of AI answers for your key questions, plus visits from AI tools in your analytics.
