FAQ

Questions, answered.

Klaviyo or Mailchimp?+

Klaviyo suits online shops; Mailchimp suits most service businesses. I'll recommend one based on what you sell.

Can I email people who never subscribed?+

No. Australia's Spam Act needs consent, the US CAN-SPAM Act needs a clear opt-out and postal address, and the Philippines' Data Privacy Act protects personal data. I keep you on the right side of all three.

How often should I email?+

A monthly newsletter plus automated emails like the welcome series is a good start. More only if you have something worth saying.

Do you write the emails?+

Yes. I write and design them, and you approve them before anything sends.
